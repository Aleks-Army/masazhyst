About

A medic who fights. A soldier who saves lives.

Masazhyst is a combat medic whose experience spans three eras of the Ukrainian military: an international peacekeeping mission, the Anti-Terrorist Operation (ATO) from 2014, and the full-scale war. On the hottest sectors of the front he remained who he is: the medic responsible for his comrades’ lives.

His experience was not earned on a training range. He delivered care on the line of fire, for weeks and months on the front line, fought as a rifleman, took part in assault operations and raids behind enemy lines. Today he is a tactical medicine instructor completing studies to teach in higher education — turning combat experience into structured knowledge.

  • 01Fire superiority and safety first, medicine second.
  • 02MARCH-PAWS, drilled until it is automatic.
  • 03Every soldier is the first medic for the person next to them.

Training

What Masazhyst teaches

Courses follow the TCCC tiers (Joint Trauma System / CoTCCC) and the levels of tactical pre-hospital care set by Ukraine’s Ministry of Defence Order No. 436. Every topic comes with examples from real combat.
